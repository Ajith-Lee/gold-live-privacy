Privacy Policy

Gold Live — Gold Price Tracker

Package: com.goldtrackerapp  |  Effective Date: November 26, 2026

Gold Live (“we”, “our”, or “the app”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information the Gold Live Android application accesses, how it is used, and your choices. Gold Live does not require account registration, login, or personal profile submission.

1. Permissions & Information We Access

  • Approximate Location (ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION): With your explicit permission, the app uses approximate (city-level) location on your device to identify your nearest supported city (for example, Chennai, Mumbai, Delhi, Bengaluru, or Hyderabad) so it can show local gold and silver rates and subscribe to the matching city price-alert topic. Precise GPS location is not requested, and your coordinates are never stored on our servers or shared with third parties. You can deny location permission and select your city manually at any time.
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS, RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED, VIBRATE): With your permission, the app sends local daily gold rate reminders and real-time push notifications when gold prices change or reach your configured alert thresholds. You can turn notifications off anytime inside the app’s Settings or in Android system settings.
  • Network Access (INTERNET): Used solely to fetch live bullion rates, historical price charts, global spot prices, and market calendar updates, and to receive push notification messages.

2. Local Storage & Third-Party Services

  • On-Device Storage: Your selected city, alert preferences, gold savings/chit tracker entries, and cached rate history are stored locally on your device using Android local storage (AsyncStorage). Uninstalling the app or clearing app data deletes this information.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM): Push notifications are delivered through Google Firebase Cloud Messaging using topic subscriptions (such as city-level gold rate topics). Firebase processes an anonymous app-instance/messaging token solely to route push notifications, in accordance with the Google Privacy Policy.

3. Data Sharing & Selling

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, contacts, photos, or payment details inside the app.

4. Children’s Privacy

Gold Live is a general-audience financial reference utility and is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect app updates or legal requirements. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

6. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how Gold Live handles data, please contact the developer at ajith.techwork@gmail.com.
